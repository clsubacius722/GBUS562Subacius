The overdraft fee is **$29 per item paid** when Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws a member's checking account. No more than three such fees apply per account per business day, and no fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at day's end (Source: consumer-fee-schedule.html).
