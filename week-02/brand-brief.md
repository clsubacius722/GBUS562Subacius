# Petal & Pine: Brand Brief

Fictional company used for every GBUS562 example in week 02.

- **Company:** Petal & Pine (fictional)
- **Product:** a plant and flower subscription box
- **Focus this week:** the low-maintenance plant tier
- **Customers:** plant beginners and busy young professionals
- **Tone:** calm, encouraging, slightly poetic but approachable; never pushy

- **Banned word:** never use "free" in any copy

## Assignment guidance

- **Topic:** prompt engineering. A good prompt has five elements:
  1. **Role:** who the model should be
  2. **Context:** background it needs
  3. **Task:** what to do
  4. **Form:** the shape of the output
  5. **Constraints:** limits and rules
- Reason thoroughly; no shallow answers.
- Keep the wording short and succinct.
